## Immersion has a new smell

You've ridden through Elwynn at sunrise. You've wept at the music of Teldrassil. You've stood in the rain in Stranglethorn and felt like you were really *there*.

But something was missing. Something deeply, fundamentally *real*.

**Brrraaaappp** fixes that. Every adventurer has a digestive system. Now your character finally acts like it.

## What it does

💨 **Your character farts.** Every now and then, at a random moment, when you least expect it. In the middle of a quest. During a long flight. Right as the raid leader is explaining the boss.

🟤 **Brown smoke.** A thick brown cloud rolls over your screen and slowly drifts away, while the edges of your screen go a little... murky. Don't worry: it stays below your UI, so you can still see your action bars while you're suffering.

🤢 **Gross flavor text written for your race and class.** Your Dwarf's beard catches fire from the fumes. Your Undead loses a piece of intestine and puts it back. Your Rogue's Stealth breaks because everyone can smell you. Your Mage blinks away, but the cloud stays behind. Every race and class has its own lines, including Skyborne.

🎭 **Share it with the world (optional).** Your character can do an `/e` emote, so everyone nearby reads things like *"Grubnak lets one rip and blames the nearest gnome."* You can switch this off with one click if you value your guild membership.

🗺️ **Nobody is innocent.** Other characters fart too: players and NPCs around you, your group, your target, even your own pet. With **directional sound**: a kobold on your left farts in your left ear, and the troll far away on the right gives a faint toot in your right ear. A small brown cloud follows their nameplate. True 3D stink. Only you hear and see this, so you can quietly judge everyone.

🎙️ **Bring your own farts.** Put your own `.ogg` or `.mp3` files in a folder, add them in the settings, and your character farts with *your* sound. We don't ask where you got it.

⚙️ **Full settings window.** Type `/brap` to turn everything on or off, set how often you and others fart, test it with **Fart now!** or **Blame someone now**, and manage your own sounds.

## Commands

- `/brap`: open the settings window
- `/brap now`: fart right now (for testing, or for comedic timing)
- `/brap on` / `/brap off`: automatic farts on or off
- `/brap status`: when is the next one coming?

## FAQ

**Can other players hear my farts?**
No. Addons can't send sound to other players. Only the optional `/e` emote is visible to others. The smoke and the gross lines are just for you.

**Do I need nameplates?**
No. Brrraaaappp hears everyone who's busy around you: fighting, casting, chatting. But it can only *see* where they are through their nameplate. Press `V` to turn nameplates on and everyone on your screen gets directional sound and their own little cloud. Your group members always sound from the right direction (outside instances).

**It's been five minutes and nothing happened!**
Patience. A good fart can't be rushed. By default it comes every 10 to 30 minutes. Check `/brap status`, or set a shorter wait in `/brap`.

**Is this lore-friendly?**
Thrall has a digestive system. Checkmate.

**How do I add my own sounds?**
Create the folder `Interface\AddOns\BrrraaaapppCustom\` (so updates don't delete your files), put your `.ogg`/`.mp3` files in it, restart WoW, then add the file name in `/brap`.
